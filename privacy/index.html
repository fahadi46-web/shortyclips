ShortyClips Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

ShortyClips is a personal tool used only by its owner to schedule and publish the owner's own short videos to the owner's own Pinterest, YouTube and TikTok accounts.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell, or share data about other people. The only data stored is the sign-in token for the owner's own connected accounts, kept securely so scheduled posts can be published, plus the videos, captions and board choices the owner uploads.

How it is used

This data is used only to publish the owner's scheduled posts and to show the owner which boards, playlists and posts exist on their own accounts.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect any account at any time, which deletes its stored token.
